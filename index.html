WHO WE ARE

Opportunity should reach further.

Olympic Capitals First Funds Foundation is focused on helping people and communities move toward stronger, more sustainable futures. Our work is guided by a simple conviction: when people have access to meaningful opportunities, they can shape lasting change for themselves and those around them.

We bring our attention to four connected areas of impact—business empowerment, community support, education and healthcare. Each reflects an essential part of a thriving society and a future in which more people can participate and prosper.

OUR PURPOSE

A future where potential
has room to grow.

Our Vision

To contribute to a world where individuals, families, entrepreneurs and communities have meaningful opportunities to achieve greater independence, access education and healthcare, and build sustainable futures. We envision communities where financial limitations do not define a person's possibilities, where ideas can become viable ventures, and where learning and wellbeing help every generation move forward.

Our Mission

To advance inclusive development by supporting initiatives that empower individuals, strengthen communities and create pathways to lasting social and economic progress. Through our four pillars, we seek to identify meaningful needs, encourage promising ideas and connect people with opportunities that can improve their circumstances.

OUR FOUR PILLARS

Four pathways. One shared purpose.

We focus on connected areas that can help individuals and communities build stronger foundations for the future.

↗

01 / ENTERPRISE

Business Empowerment

We support the ambition and initiative of entrepreneurs and small businesses. By encouraging access to resources, development opportunities and practical support, we aim to help ideas take shape, enterprises become more resilient and livelihoods grow.

◎

02 / PEOPLE

Community Support

Strong communities are built when people can participate in shaping their shared future. We seek to encourage initiatives that respond to community needs, strengthen inclusion and contribute to safer, more resilient and more connected places to live.

▤

03 / LEARNING

Education

Education can unlock opportunity across a lifetime. We value initiatives that widen access to learning, strengthen educational participation and equip learners with knowledge and skills to pursue their ambitions and contribute to society.

✚

04 / WELLBEING

Healthcare

Health and wellbeing are central to individual dignity and community progress. We aim to support initiatives that improve access to healthcare, promote awareness and contribute to healthier, more informed communities.

LEADERSHIP

Guided by purpose and responsibility.

Our leadership roles are focused on strategic direction, financial stewardship, programme coordination and responsible grant administration.

CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER

Ronny Goodman

The Chief Executive Officer provides overall strategic leadership and direction for the Foundation. The role guides organizational priorities, oversees the execution of its mission and goals, and helps ensure that programmes and operations remain aligned with the Foundation's values. The CEO also supports leadership coordination, represents the Foundation in organizational matters and promotes accountability across its work.

CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER

Edward Fischer

The Chief Financial Officer oversees financial planning, budgeting, financial reporting and the responsible management of the Foundation's resources. The role supports sound financial controls, monitors expenditure against approved plans and helps ensure that funds are managed transparently and in accordance with programme objectives and internal procedures.

VICE PRESIDENT OF THE PROGRAMME

Edmundson Fischer

The Vice President of the Programme supports the development, coordination and delivery of the Foundation's programmes. The role helps translate strategic priorities into practical activities, coordinates programme implementation across the four pillars, monitors progress and supports collaboration among the teams and stakeholders involved in delivering programme objectives.

DIRECTOR OF GRANTS MANAGEMENT

Donald McIlroy

The Director of Grants Management oversees the administration and coordination of grant applications and awards. The role supports the application review process, maintains grant records, coordinates communication with applicants, monitors grant-related timelines and helps ensure that decisions and grant administration follow established procedures with consistency, fairness and appropriate documentation.
